# Security Policy

Batwise develops in private repositories. This policy covers our products and
services — the Batwise platform (`app.batwise.ai`), the marketing site
(`batwise.ai`), and the tracking pixel we host for customers.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **sebastian@batwise.ai** with:

- what you found and where (URL, endpoint, or parameter),
- the steps to reproduce it,
- what an attacker could do with it.

Please report privately first and give us a chance to fix it before publishing.
Do not open a public GitHub issue for a security problem.

## What we ask

- Test only against accounts and data you own. Do not access, modify, or exfiltrate
  another customer's data — if you stumble into it, stop and tell us.
- No denial of service, spam, social engineering, or physical attacks.
- Stay within the scope above. Our customers' own websites are not in scope.

## What you can expect

We will confirm we received your report, tell you whether we consider it a
vulnerability, and keep you updated while we work on a fix. We are a small team,
so we would rather set expectations when we reply than promise a fixed clock here.
We are happy to credit you once the issue is resolved, if you want that.

We do not currently run a paid bug bounty.
